Wordmark · study 1

The word, drawn from the mark.

A lowercase, single-weight wordmark built from the 5a mark's own parts: its circular bowl, its sighting rule, its angle and its blue stem. This is direction A, the one I recommend. Two alternates are at the end for comparison.

What I need from you

Approve A, or tell me what to change. Once it's approved I'll add it to the brand kit, swap it into the website and write the usage page.

Construction

Nothing in it is new. Every part comes from the mark.

  • One bowld, e, c and b share one circle, the same size as the mark's bowl in the lockup.
  • The e's bar is the sighting ruleIt is the bowl's horizontal diameter, exactly as the rule sits in the mark.
  • Openings follow the sight angleThe e opens from its bar to θ below it; the c opens θ either side. The s and r end on θ too.
  • The caret is the mark's stemThe blue bar after the bracket is the mark's blue stem, and the name's controlled form: .describe(|. It is 1.4× the letter stroke.
  • Tall ascendersThe d and b rise 1.74× the x-height, the same proportion as the mark's stem to its bowl.
  • The dot and the endsThe leading dot keeps the mark's dot-to-stroke ratio. Every stroke ends round, like the mark's.
Two weights

A light cut for large use, a heavier cut for small.

They pair with the mark's own tiers: primary with the primary mark, compact with the compact mark used for favicons, avatars and the site's nav.

Lockups

Horizontal for most things, stacked for squares.

The stacked lockup sets the mark at twice the horizontal scale, where its ring roughly matches the letters’ stroke and its stem the caret.

Rules

Use it as artwork, never as typing.

  • Clear spaceKeep one x-height clear on every side of the lockup, measured from the ring and the caret.
  • ColoursNavy letters with a blue caret; off-white with light blue on navy; or a single colour throughout. Nothing else.
  • In Arabic textAlways place the SVG. Typed into a right-to-left line, .describe(| gets reordered and the dot and bracket jump ends. Typed handles go in a left-to-right span.
  • Don't retype the name in a font to stand in for the wordmark.
  • Don't stretch, outline, add shadows or change the spacing.
  • Don't colour any part coral. Coral is for data outliers only.
  • Don't use the primary cut small or the compact cut large.
  • Don't put the caret back in blue when the rest is one colour.
Tested

What an independent critic checked, and what changed.

TestResultWhat changed
OriginalityFixedThe first draft sat too close to Quicksand, a free rounded font. Heavier stroke, tighter spacing, taller ascenders and a wider s set it apart.
The sFixedRebuilt as two circles joined by a straight tangent spine, then widened to about 0.7 of a bowl.
Blur testFixedThe caret out-weighed the word. Cut from 1.7× to 1.4× the letter stroke, with more space before it.
e and iFixedThe e's stroke now ends flat on its bar; the i-dot sits higher and larger.
Small sizesPassCompact reads from 20px up. At 16px the bracket and caret merge, hence the minimum size.
One colour and reversedPassHolds on navy, white, off-white and in black.
Unfortunate readingsPassNone found in English or Arabic contexts. In one colour, (| can read as (l, which is why the caret keeps its space.
