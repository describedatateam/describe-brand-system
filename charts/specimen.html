Floors: ΔE 15 under normal vision, ΔE 8 under protanopia and deuteranopia. The validator also flags context grey's low chroma (0.041 light, 0.010 dark) on every run. For identity colours that is a failure; for context it is the job. So the palette carries one story series per chart. Two or more series that each need an identity are small multiples, or a palette extension — a decision for the brand, not for a chart.

Text contrast, WCAG: dim ink #63738D 4.52:1 on the ground (was #8A99B0, 2.89:1 — fixed across the kit). Coral as text: 3.91:1. It fails, so coral is never text.

04

Rules

Each one is here because a chart on this page, or in an earlier role, broke it first.

  1. The title is the finding. Not the topic. “S&P 500 returns” is a label; “Three months in 32 years fell past 3σ” is a chart.
  2. One story series. Blue for the series the finding is about, grey for context. The palette cannot carry more identities than that — measured, §02.
  3. Every chart states n. With the source and the date computed, in the provenance line. No n, no chart.
  4. Every outlier states its test. Coral appears only with the rule that flagged it. Where a rule flags nothing, the chart says so — Fig. 04.
  5. Name the rule, because rules disagree. |z| > 3 flags three months of S&P returns; a 3×IQR fence flags none. The choice is part of the finding.
  6. Four data states, four drawings. Missing (hatch, broken line) · not applicable (capped start) · null (em dash) · structural empty (removed, counted in provenance).
  7. Never manufacture a gap. If the data has no missing values, the missing indicator appears in the key only. Fig. 02.
  8. Dashed means threshold. Benchmarks, ±3σ, projections. Gridlines are always solid.
  9. Text never wears a data colour. Labels are ink or body; the swatch beside them carries identity. Coral text fails contrast anyway.
  10. Colour follows meaning, not sign. Negative red for a value that moved the bad way. Never mix status colours with identity colours in one chart.
  11. One axis. Two scales on one plot invent a correlation. Index to a common base instead — Fig. 01.
  12. Every chart has a table twin. Tooltips enhance, never gate. Every value is reachable in the table view.
  13. Mono is for numerals and short labels. Tick values, n, measurements. Annotations are sentences, so they are sans.
  14. Association, said as association. A fitted slope is not an effect. The dek says which it is.
