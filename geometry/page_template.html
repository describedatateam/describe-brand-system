The 5a astrolabe is not an assembly of parts. Fix the sight axis θ and everything else is forced: the star sits on the ring at θ, the dot at θ+180°, the bowl is tangent exactly at that dot, and the aperture opens where the instrument is looking. This is the vocabulary that falls out of it — 46 primitives, all derived, none invented.

The derivation

Four numbers. Every other measurement in the mark is a consequence of them, which is why the reconstruction in this page matches the original file exactly. If a future edit breaks that match, the constants are wrong — not the logo.

ElementForced by
Starsits on the ring at θ — a sighting target, not an ornament
Positional dotthe ring at θ + 180°, antipodal to the star
Bowl centreon the sight axis, at R − r = 44 from the ring centre
Tangencythe bowl touches the ring exactly at the dot — one point does two jobs
Aperture28° centred on θ: the instrument is open where it looks
Star phaseθ mod 45° = 4.4°, so one of the eight arms lies along θ
Sighting raythat arm, extended to 30 units
Stemthe vertical tangent to the bowl at its rightmost point
Horizontal rulethe bowl's own diameter, terminating at that tangent

02 · the library

Forty-six primitives

Extracted from the astrolabe, not illustrations of one. The two switches above are tests 06 and 07 from the design brief, made operable: collapse every ink to one and the drawing should survive; delete the whole faint layer and the information should still read.

03 · guards

Rules the code enforces

raises Graduation is never uniform all the way round

radial_scale() refuses a sweep of 330° or more. A fully graduated circle reads as a clock; a graduated sector reads as an instrument. Asymmetry is the entire difference.

raises A closed ring is not a ring

broken_ring() refuses gap ≤ 0. If you want a plain circle, call circle() and know you have stepped outside the vocabulary.

never automatic Coral is always a judgement

No primitive reaches for the mark ink on its own. data_series() takes an explicit list of outlier indices, so coral only ever appears because someone decided a point mattered.

no raw hex Five semantic inks, everywhere

ink · body · faint · active · mark. That is what makes light, dark and single-ink the same drawing rather than three drawings — and what makes the switches above possible at all.

04 · before approving anything

The design test

  1. 01
    Could this belong to a generic AI startup? If yes, redesign it.
  2. 02
    Is there a real relationship to measurement, information, orientation, computation or knowledge? If no, remove it.
  3. 03
    Does it use the vocabulary without drawing an astrolabe? If yes, good.
  4. 04
    Is coral saying something? If no, remove it.
  5. 05
    Would it still read as .describe( with the logo removed? If no, the system is too logo-dependent.
  6. 06
    Does it survive in one colour? Set all five inks to the same value and look again — the switch above does it.
  7. 07
    Is the information still clear with the decorative layer gone? Delete every faint element and check.
