Privacy

This site has no accounts, no analytics scripts and no advertising trackers.

When you contact us

When you send the form on the homepage, what you type (name, email, position, organisation and message) is saved to a private Google Sheet that only we can open, and we get an email alert at info@describe.team.

We use what you send only to reply to you and, if we work together, to run that project. We don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Data you share for a project

Files you send us for analysis are used for that project only, kept no longer than the project needs, and deleted on request.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and form submissions are handled by Google Apps Script.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, write to info@describe.team.
